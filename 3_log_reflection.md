AI Audit and Reflection

1. AI Interpretation

The mean represents the arithmetic average, while the median represents the middle value of the observations.

An unusually high price can increase the mean considerably, while the median may remain relatively stable. Therefore, both statistics should be considered when interpreting price data.

3. Confused Classmate Explanation

The mean and median both describe the centre of a dataset, but they work differently. The mean is calculated by adding all prices and dividing by the number of observations. The median is the middle price after arranging the observations in order.

For example, if a dataset contains an unusually high price, the mean may increase significantly. The median may change very little. This is why the median can sometimes provide a better representation of a typical price.

4. Reflection

I initially focused on calculating averages without fully considering how unusual observations could affect the results. I learned that the mean and median can provide different interpretations of the same dataset. I also learned that descriptive statistics show patterns but cannot independently establish the reasons behind price changes.
